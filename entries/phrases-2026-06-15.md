# Phrases and Collocations - 2026-06-15

## commiserate with sb
- meaning: 对某人表示同情；与某人共鸣并安慰对方
- example: I called my friend to commiserate with him after his interview went badly.
- usage: 描述因共同经历挫折而安慰或同情某人
- tags: speaking, daily
- date: 2026-06-15

## on the verge of
- meaning: 即将；濒临；处于……的边缘
- example: The company was on the verge of collapse before it secured new funding.
- usage: 描述即将发生的变化、突破、危机或情绪状态
- tags: writing, speaking
- date: 2026-06-15

## be wired for
- meaning: 天生倾向于；大脑或本能被设定为适合……
- example: Human beings are wired for social connection.
- usage: 描述人的本能、思维模式或生理倾向
- tags: writing, psychology
- date: 2026-06-15

## judge the trade-offs
- meaning: 权衡利弊；判断不同选择之间的取舍
- example: Policymakers need reliable evidence to judge the trade-offs.
- usage: 决策、政策、投资或方案比较
- tags: writing, decision
- date: 2026-06-15

## leave ... intact
- meaning: 使……保持完整；不损害或不改变……
- example: The renovation modernized the building while leaving its historic character intact.
- usage: 描述改变部分内容但保留核心结构、功能或特征
- tags: writing, academic
- date: 2026-06-15

## be insulated from
- meaning: 与……隔绝；不受……影响或冲击
- example: Wealthy households are often better insulated from sudden price increases.
- usage: 经济、社会、风险及环境影响分析
- tags: writing, academic
- date: 2026-06-15

## reflects a broader tendency
- meaning: 反映一种更广泛的趋势或倾向
- example: This change reflects a broader tendency towards flexible working.
- usage: 议论文、研究分析或社会趋势描述
- tags: writing, academic
- date: 2026-06-15

## long-term social consequences
- meaning: 长期社会后果
- example: The policy may have long-term social consequences that are difficult to reverse.
- usage: 政策、科技、教育或社会问题的长期影响
- tags: writing, academic
- date: 2026-06-15

## controversy surrounding
- meaning: 围绕……的争议
- example: The controversy surrounding the proposal has intensified in recent weeks.
- usage: 引出新闻、政策、科技或社会议题中的争议
- tags: writing, news
- date: 2026-06-15

## emerge in a context
- meaning: 在某种背景下出现或形成
- example: New social norms often emerge in a context of rapid technological change.
- usage: 解释观点、制度、趋势或问题产生的背景
- tags: writing, academic
- date: 2026-06-15

## be treated as urgent
- meaning: 被视为紧急事项；需要优先处理
- example: The shortage of affordable housing should be treated as urgent.
- usage: 政策建议、问题解决及强调优先级
- tags: writing, academic
- date: 2026-06-15

## be tempted to
- meaning: 忍不住想；受到诱惑而想做……
- example: When progress is slow, people may be tempted to give up.
- usage: 描述冲动、诱惑或容易作出的选择
- tags: speaking, writing
- date: 2026-06-15

## Were it not for
- meaning: 若不是；要不是
- example: Were it not for public transport, many residents could not reach the city centre.
- usage: 正式倒装条件句，用于说明某因素的重要作用
- tags: grammar, writing
- date: 2026-06-15
