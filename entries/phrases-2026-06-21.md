# Phrases and Collocations - 2026-06-21

## has a leg up
- meaning: 有优势；占据有利位置
- example: Candidates with strong writing samples often have a leg up in the application process.
- usage: 描述竞争、申请、工作、考试或市场中的相对优势
- tags: speaking, competition
- date: 2026-06-21

## when it comes to doing
- meaning: 当谈到做某事；在做某事方面
- example: When it comes to managing stress, regular sleep matters more than people think.
- usage: 引出某个具体领域、能力或评价角度
- tags: writing, speaking
- date: 2026-06-21

## stumble upon
- meaning: 偶然发现；无意中碰到
- example: I stumbled upon a useful article while looking for IELTS examples.
- usage: 描述非计划性的发现、遇见或获得信息
- tags: daily, reading
- date: 2026-06-21

## lit up
- meaning: 亮起来；变得兴奋或开心；被照亮
- example: Her face lit up when she heard the good news.
- usage: 描述表情、情绪、屏幕、城市或场景突然变亮或活跃
- tags: speaking, description
- date: 2026-06-21

## rile up
- meaning: 激怒；煽动；让人情绪激动
- example: The comment riled up the crowd and made the debate more hostile.
- usage: 描述言论、新闻、争议或挑衅行为引发强烈情绪
- tags: speaking, news
- date: 2026-06-21

## on the fence
- meaning: 犹豫不决；还没有站队或做决定
- example: I am still on the fence about whether to change my major.
- usage: 表达对选择、观点、购买或决定仍在观望
- tags: speaking, decision
- date: 2026-06-21

## ripped off
- meaning: 被宰；被坑；被敲竹杠
- example: I felt ripped off after paying so much for such poor service.
- usage: 描述价格不公、服务差、被骗钱或感觉不值
- tags: speaking, daily
- date: 2026-06-21

## flipping cars
- meaning: 掀翻汽车；把车翻过来
- example: The protest turned violent, with some people flipping cars in the street.
- usage: 描述骚乱、暴力场面或极端混乱的街头行为
- tags: news, action
- date: 2026-06-21

## is a critical determinant of
- meaning: 是……的关键决定因素
- example: Access to quality education is a critical determinant of long-term income.
- usage: 学术写作中说明某因素对结果具有决定性影响
- tags: academic, writing
- date: 2026-06-21
