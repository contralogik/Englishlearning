# Phrases and Collocations - 2026-09-23

## be torn about sth
- meaning: 对某事犹豫不决；内心很纠结
- example: I'm torn about whether to accept the offer.
- usage: 在两个选择之间难以决定
- tags: speaking, daily
- date: 2026-09-23

## hold out
- meaning: 坚持下去；支撑住；抵抗
- example: The team held out until help arrived.
- usage: 描述在困难或压力下继续坚持
- tags: speaking, writing
- date: 2026-09-23

## get to do sth
- meaning: 有机会做某事；得以做某事
- example: I finally got to meet the author in person.
- usage: 表达获得做某事的机会
- tags: speaking, daily
- date: 2026-09-23

## be crazy about sth
- meaning: 对某事非常热衷；特别喜欢某事
- example: My brother is crazy about basketball.
- usage: 口语中表达对爱好或事物的强烈喜爱
- tags: speaking, daily
- date: 2026-09-23

## level sb up
- meaning: 提升某人的能力或水平
- example: This project could level me up as a writer.
- usage: 非正式表达，常用于技能成长或游戏语境
- tags: speaking, learning
- date: 2026-09-23

## run into sth
- meaning: 偶然遇到或碰到某事；遇到问题
- example: I ran into a few unfamiliar words while reading the article.
- usage: 描述偶然遇见某事、碰到问题或遇到生词
- tags: speaking, reading
- date: 2026-09-23

## come across sth
- meaning: 偶然发现或碰到某事
- example: I came across several useful expressions in the essay.
- usage: 阅读或搜索时偶然发现信息、表达或物品
- tags: speaking, reading
- date: 2026-09-23
